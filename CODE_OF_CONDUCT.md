# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this to be a welcoming space for people opening their first pull request ever. Everyone is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be patient — this is a learning environment. Questions that seem "obvious" are welcome.
- Give constructive feedback in reviews. Explain *why*, not just *what's wrong*.
- Assume good intent, especially in text where tone is easy to misread.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or dismissive comments toward any contributor.
- Gatekeeping — telling someone they "shouldn't be contributing" because they're new.
- Deliberately submitting low-effort or spam PRs to farm contribution counts.

## Enforcement

Issues can be reported to the session facilitators. Violations may result in comments being removed or contributors being blocked from the repo.
